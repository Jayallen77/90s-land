Type

The editorial interface uses locally hosted Jersey 10, Barlow, and Barlow Condensed from the Google Fonts repository. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included: Jersey 10, Barlow, and Barlow Condensed.

The sharing card uses Press Start 2P and Space Mono under their Press Start 2P and Space Mono licenses. These fonts are part of the image, not the page interface.

Editorial status

Verified artifacts use a source trail. Editorial objects are clearly labeled original recreations. Items marked needs source are excluded from Surprise Me and guided tours.

Image adaptations

Photographs are locally resized and may be cropped by the page layout. Credits name the original creators and link to the original file records; Creative Commons ShareAlike terms continue to apply to adapted images. Original interface recreations are labeled separately. The Y2K office photograph is credited to the Government of Japan, Prime Minister’s Office website, under its Standard Terms of Use 2.0, compatible with CC BY 4.0.

Sharing artwork

The Home, 1996 Timeline, Games, Music, Movies & TV, Tech, and Culture hero collages are original AI-generated editorial illustrations created with OpenAI on September 26–27, 2026. They evoke the decade and are not documentary photographs or evidence of release dates. Brands and illustrated products belong to their respective owners. The palm/sunset brand mark and interface icons are original SVG artwork.

The sharing card combines an original AI-generated museum-case illustration created with OpenAI and lettering in Press Start 2P and Space Mono.
