Diablo opens the cathedral door

Diablo turns a descent beneath Tristram into a loop of exploration, combat and another piece of loot.

Dec 31, 1996 · Original release — publisher retrospective

The town is a breathing space; the stairs are an invitation to leave it again. Diablo builds its rhythm around returning to the darkness, finding equipment and deciding whether a character is ready to go farther. Randomly generated spaces help make the next descent feel unsettled even after the route becomes familiar.

Blizzard dates the original release to December 31, 1996. That places this PC landmark at the very edge of the year, rather than in the later period associated with its sequels. Its connected play belongs in the archive beside the physical work of getting home computers to communicate. The later GOG edition is a preservation story, not the launch recorded here.

About this date: Blizzard retrospectively identifies this day as Diablo’s original release; later editions and platform releases are separate.
