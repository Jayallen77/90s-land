Depeche Mode releases Violator

Personal Jesus and Enjoy the Silence share a place on the same record.

Mar 19, 1990 · United Kingdom

Depeche Mode’s archive dates the original UK LP to March 19. Its track listing brings together Personal Jesus, Enjoy the Silence, and the quieter Waiting for the Night. The cassette in this collection represents listening and copying habits, rather than a particular pressing.

About this date: Original UK commercial LP, March 19, 1990; later reissues have separate dates.
