When the game became a shared moment

Sport gave the decade dates that traveled far beyond the scoreboard: the Bulls’ first NBA title, South Africa’s Rugby World Cup, Muhammad Ali at the Atlanta opening ceremony, the WNBA’s first game, and the 1999 Women’s World Cup final. A result, a photograph, and a broadcast could carry different parts of the same occasion into everyday conversation.

An opening ceremony, a final, and the beginning of a professional league are different kinds of milestones. Their dates give each occasion a place in the decade, while the sources keep the actual people, teams, and regional context visible.
