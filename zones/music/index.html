Mixtape cassette

About this object

Pause-button edits and handwritten track lists made every tape a small act of authorship.

Why it mattered: The mixtape made music discovery physical, social, and intentionally sequenced.

portable CD ritual · 1990–1999

Sony Discman

About this object

A careful walk, a pocket full of jewel cases, and anti-skip optimism created a personal soundtrack outside the house.

Why it mattered: Portable CD players made album ownership mobile while keeping the format tactile.

About this object

The rainbow discs waited to become a summer mix, a backup, or a folder of badly labeled MP3s.

Why it mattered: CD burners shifted music collections from sealed packages toward personal copying and sequencing.
