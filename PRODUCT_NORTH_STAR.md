# 90s.land Product North Star

## North Star

**90s.land should feel like a playable museum of the 1990s.**

Browse the decade. Open its objects. Get lost on purpose.

## Target visitor

Curious people who remember the 1990s, people who only know the decade through
its cultural afterlife, and friends following a shared nostalgia link. Visitors
should understand the premise and find something to do within ten seconds.

## Core experience loop

1. Enter the Museum Desk.
2. Start the guided tour, open a room, or choose Surprise Me.
3. Inspect an artifact and learn one concise, sourced or clearly editorial fact.
4. Add a stamp or artifact to the local Museum Passport.
5. Follow a related object, year, room, or cultural connection.
6. Return later with harmless progress preserved on the same device.

## Design principles

- Objects before essays; longer context remains available progressively.
- Discovery over completion; progress should invite curiosity, not create a grind.
- Retro flavor with contemporary navigation, responsiveness, and accessibility.
- Motion and audio are optional; nothing flashes, autoplays, or impersonates a
  system warning.
- Facts, editorial interpretation, recreations, and community memories are
  labeled honestly.
- Controls work, are explicitly decorative, or are removed.
- Existing writing, URLs, visual identity, and licensed media remain the
  foundation.

## Out of scope for this share-ready pass

- Accounts, profiles, analytics, or personal-data collection.
- A persistent public guestbook or any unmoderated community submission system.
- Autoplay or optional sound.
- More than the single “Before the Feed” guided tour.
- Migrating every long-form passage or every existing object card into data.
- A framework or static-site-generator migration.
- Production deployment, VPS changes, DNS changes, or active Cloudflare changes.
