THE DECADE, DAY BY DAY

1991

Turn it up. Follow the blue link.

Nevermind and Ten arrived a few weeks apart. Sonic raced across the television, the Bulls won their first title, and a Usenet post invited strangers to try the Web.

Defining moments ↓Full calendar ↓Objects ↓Stories ↓

10 defining moments

Start here for a taste of 1991, then follow any date into its sources and connections.

  1. Jun 12, 1991 · Sports

    The Bulls win their first NBA title

    Chicago beats the Lakers to finish the Finals.

  2. Jun 23, 1991 · Games

    Sonic races onto the Mega Drive and Genesis

    Sega’s blue hedgehog makes speed part of the console rivalry.

  3. Jul 2, 1991 · Movies

    Terminator 2 returns for the summer

    The sequel opens during the July cinema season.

  4. Aug 6, 1991 · Internet

    The Web gets a public invitation

    Tim Berners-Lee posts about the WorldWideWeb project on Usenet.

  5. Aug 11, 1991 · Television

    Rugrats sees the world from the carpet

    Tommy Pickles and his friends join Nickelodeon’s first Nicktoons.

  6. Aug 12, 1991 · Music

    Metallica releases the Black Album

    Metallica’s self-titled album arrives on the band’s official release calendar.

  7. Aug 27, 1991 · Music

    Pearl Jam releases Ten

    Alive, Even Flow, and Jeremy arrive on the band’s debut album.

  8. Sep 24, 1991 · Music

    Nirvana releases Nevermind

    Nirvana releases Nevermind. The artist’s official discography supplies the release date.

  9. Nov 13, 1991 · Movies

    Beauty and the Beast opens in November

    A Disney animation joins the autumn theatrical calendar.

  10. Nov 21, 1991 · Games

    A Link to the Past opens two Hyrules

    Link’s Super Famicom adventure begins in Japan.

The first World Wide Web site

About this object

The first site looks almost modest: text, blue links, and an explanation of what the web is supposed to be.

It established the linked-document grammar that everything else in the museum builds on.
