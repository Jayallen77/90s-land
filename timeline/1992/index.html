pocket-status beep · 1992–1998

Alphanumeric pager

About this object

The pager goes off. Finding a phone is your next problem.

The TELTRIM-branded alphanumeric pager in the reference photograph receives short messages on a compact display. Its visible badge does not establish a Motorola model. An alert can reach the wearer away from a desk without supplying the full two-way conversation of a telephone.

clickable beige future · 1990

Windows 3.0 Program Manager

About this object

Colorful icons made the family PC feel less like office machinery and more like something worth exploring.

Windows 3.0, released in 1990, organizes applications through Program Manager. Groups contain icons; opening an icon launches the associated program. The screen here was captured in a later virtual-machine session, preserving an identified software environment rather than claiming to be a photograph made in 1990.

public confidence test · 1990–1999

Arcade cabinet

About this object

Leave a quarter on the cabinet. Somebody else is playing your next game.

This Marble Madness cabinet joins a dedicated game, screen and trackball controls inside one piece of furniture. The design begins before the 1990s, while continued play belongs to the longer life of arcade machines. Its control surface is specific to the game’s movement problem.

Halcyanide ↗ · CC BY-SA 4.0

portable CD ritual · 1990–1999

Sony Discman

About this object

Pick a track, close the lid, and try not to bump the player on the walk home.

The pictured Sony D-121 is a portable compact-disc player. Its clamshell protects a full-size disc while transport controls let the listener select tracks, pause and stop without moving through tape first. It is a playback machine: a stack of favorite CDs becomes portable through the player, headphones and a suitable source of power.

Morn at English Wikipedia ↗ · CC BY-SA 3.0
