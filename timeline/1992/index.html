THE DECADE, DAY BY DAY

1992

Seven strangers and a second controller

MTV moved strangers into a New York home; Carson said goodnight. Barcelona filled summer television, Mario Kart invited a second player, and Whitney Houston led a soundtrack that outlived the cinema trip.
