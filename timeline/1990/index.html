THE DECADE, DAY BY DAY

1990

A remote, a cassette, a new desktop

Twin Peaks gave Sunday night a mystery. Windows offered icons to click, and Mario found Yoshi in Japan. Beyond the living room, Mandela walked free and Germany reunited.

Defining moments ↓Full calendar ↓Objects ↓Stories ↓

  1. Feb 11, 1990 · World news

    Nelson Mandela walks free

  2. Mar 19, 1990 · Music

clickable beige future · 1990

Windows 3.0 Program Manager

About this object

Colorful icons made the family PC feel less like office machinery and more like something worth exploring.

A friendlier visual interface helped normalize home computing before the mass web.

the future as a launch plume · 1990

Hubble launch

About this object

Discovery carries Hubble out of Florida and into orbit.

The launch on April 24, 1990 began a mission that would need repairs as well as produce new views of space. The launch and deployment happened on separate days.

NASA / ESA ↗ · Public domain

Mixtape cassette

About this object

Pause-button edits and handwritten track lists made every tape a small act of authorship.

The mixtape made music discovery physical, social, and intentionally sequenced.

the whole night on one tape · 1990–1999

VHS videocassette

About this object

The label says DO NOT TAPE OVER. Someone in the house means it.

The same cassette could hold a rental, a favorite episode, or a home recording. Labels and the record-protect tab helped distinguish what could be replaced.
