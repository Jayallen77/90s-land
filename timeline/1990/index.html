A remote, a cassette, a new desktop

clickable beige future · 1990

Windows 3.0 Program Manager

About this object

Colorful icons made the family PC feel less like office machinery and more like something worth exploring.

Windows 3.0, released in 1990, organizes applications through Program Manager. Groups contain icons; opening an icon launches the associated program. The screen here was captured in a later virtual-machine session, preserving an identified software environment rather than claiming to be a photograph made in 1990.

the future as a launch plume · 1990

Hubble launch

About this object

Discovery carries Hubble out of Florida and into orbit.

Discovery carried the Hubble Space Telescope into orbit on STS-31 in April 1990. Launch and telescope deployment are separate milestones: the shuttle lifted off on April 24, and Hubble was deployed on April 25. This image shows departure from Earth, not a view made by the telescope.

NASA / ESA ↗ · Public domain

hand-built sequence · 1990–1997

Mixtape cassette

About this object

Pause-button edits and handwritten track lists made every tape a small act of authorship.

A compact cassette encloses magnetic tape on two reels. Playback moves that tape past a head; recording writes a new signal along it. The shell and label turn a length of sound into a portable object with two named sides.

Andrey Korzun ↗ · CC BY-SA 4.0

the whole night on one tape · 1990–1999

VHS videocassette

About this object

The label says DO NOT TAPE OVER. Someone in the house means it.

A VHS cassette carries magnetic videotape on two reels. Its front flap and shell protect the material during ordinary handling; a compatible deck draws the tape through a mechanism to read or record it. The label is the fastest way to know what should be inside.

LoMit ↗ · CC BY-SA 4.0
