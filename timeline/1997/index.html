THE DECADE, DAY BY DAY

1997

Big adventures, new voices, one more screen name

Final Fantasy VII filled PlayStation discs; Homework filled dance floors. The WNBA played its first game, Pathfinder reached Mars, and Hong Kong’s handover reached midnight. A cartoon town in Colorado joined late-night cable.
