THE DECADE, DAY BY DAY

1997

Big adventures, new voices, one more screen name

Final Fantasy VII filled PlayStation discs; Homework filled dance floors. The WNBA played its first game, Pathfinder reached Mars, and Hong Kong’s handover reached midnight. A cartoon town in Colorado joined late-night cable.

burn-your-own future · 1997–1999

Blank CD-R spindle

About this object

The rainbow discs waited to become a summer mix, a backup, or a folder of badly labeled MP3s.

A spindle of CD-Rs represents a supply of recordable optical discs rather than a finished music collection. A compatible writer and suitable software could put files or audio onto a disc, turning an apparently interchangeable blank into a particular copy with a particular purpose.

portable CD ritual · 1990–1999

Sony Discman

About this object

Pick a track, close the lid, and try not to bump the player on the walk home.

A portable CD player puts the disc mechanism, controls, and headphone connection inside an object that can travel. The Sony Discman shown here has its lid closed; the exterior, rather than an exposed disc, is the actual subject of the photograph. Opening it would be the practical first step in choosing what to play.

the console grows up · 1994–1999

Original PlayStation hardware

About this object

Put the disc in. Keep the memory card somewhere you can find it.

Sony’s original PlayStation has a top-loading disc lid, two controller ports, and separate slots for memory cards. These openings reveal different jobs: the disc supplies the game, the controller supplies the player’s actions, and the card can preserve progress after the machine is switched off.
