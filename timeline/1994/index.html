THE DECADE, DAY BY DAY

1994

The coffee shop and the loading screen

Friends and ER joined the weekly television routine. Dookie grew into a hit, Brazil won a penalty shootout, and Japan got its first PlayStation. South Africa held its first democratic election; Britain and France opened a tunnel.

Defining moments ↓Full calendar ↓Objects ↓Stories ↓

10 defining moments

Start here for a taste of 1994, then follow any date into its sources and connections.

  1. Feb 1, 1994 · Music

    Green Day releases Dookie

    A short, loud record brings Bay Area punk to a major label.

  2. Apr 27, 1994 · World news

    Nelson Mandela casts his first democratic vote

    Mandela votes in South Africa’s first democratic elections.

  3. May 6, 1994 · Technology

    The Channel Tunnel officially opens

    The inauguration links Britain and France beneath the Channel.

  4. Jul 6, 1994 · Movies

    Forrest Gump opens in July

    A summer opening on the North American theatrical calendar.

  5. Jul 17, 1994 · Sports

    Brazil wins the World Cup on penalties

    The final at the Rose Bowl reaches a shootout after a goalless match.

  6. Sep 19, 1994 · Television

    ER begins a busy night in Chicago

    The two-hour pilot sends NBC viewers into County General.

  7. Sep 22, 1994 · Television

    Friends opens the door to Central Perk

    Six friends join NBC’s Thursday-night lineup.

  8. Oct 1, 1994 · Internet

    The Web gets a standards consortium

    W3C forms to coordinate a Web that can work across different systems.

  9. Oct 14, 1994 · Movies

    Pulp Fiction reaches the autumn screen

    The October opening puts it in a different season from the summer blockbusters.

  10. Dec 3, 1994 · Games

    PlayStation launches in Japan

    Sony’s first PlayStation arrives in its home market.
