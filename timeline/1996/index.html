1996

POCKET MONSTERS. BROWSER INBOXES.

A new controller on the couch.
A tiny pet asking to be fed.
Let’s open the year.

Pokémon began in Japan, Nintendo 64 arrived on both sides of the Pacific, and Tamagotchi asked for attention. Hotmail put email in the browser. Wannabe made an entrance, and Muhammad Ali lit the Olympic cauldron.

three-prong multiplayer sculpture · 1996–1999

Gray Nintendo 64 controller

About this object

Its odd shape and analog stick announced that 3D games would need a different kind of touch.

Nintendo’s original N64 controller offers three grips, a central control stick, a directional cross and buttons in several locations. The three handles propose alternative hand positions; they do not ask for a third hand. Different games make different parts of the pad important.

Evan-Amos ↗ · Public domain

the console grows up · 1994–1999

Original PlayStation hardware

About this object

Put the disc in. Keep the memory card somewhere you can find it.

Sony’s original PlayStation separates three jobs across the front and lid: the disc contains software, the controller supplies input and a memory card holds compatible saved data. Two controller ports support local play when the game offers it.

Evan-Amos ↗ · Public domain

the whole night on one tape · 1990–1999

VHS videocassette

About this object

The label says DO NOT TAPE OVER. Someone in the house means it.

A VHS cassette carries magnetic videotape on two reels. Its front flap and shell protect the material during ordinary handling; a compatible deck draws the tape through a mechanism to read or record it. The label is the fastest way to know what should be inside.

LoMit ↗ · CC BY-SA 4.0

portable CD ritual · 1990–1999

Sony Discman

About this object

Pick a track, close the lid, and try not to bump the player on the walk home.

The pictured Sony D-121 is a portable compact-disc player. Its clamshell protects a full-size disc while transport controls let the listener select tracks, pause and stop without moving through tape first. It is a playback machine: a stack of favorite CDs becomes portable through the player, headphones and a suitable source of power.

Morn at English Wikipedia ↗ · CC BY-SA 3.0
