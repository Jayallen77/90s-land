1996

POCKET MONSTERS. BROWSER INBOXES.

A new controller on the couch.
A tiny pet asking to be fed.
Let’s open the year.

Pokémon began in Japan, Nintendo 64 arrived on both sides of the Pacific, and Tamagotchi asked for attention. Hotmail put email in the browser. Wannabe made an entrance, and Muhammad Ali lit the Olympic cauldron.

Defining moments ↓Full calendar ↓Objects ↓Stories ↓

11 defining moments

Start here for a taste of 1996, then follow any date into its sources and connections.

  1. Feb 27, 1996 · Games

    Pokémon Red and Green begin the journey

    The original Pokémon games launch for Game Boy in Japan.

  2. Jun 22, 1996 · Games

    Quake opens the Slipgate

    id Software’s Quake brings its 3D world to the PC.

  3. Jun 23, 1996 · Games

    Nintendo 64 arrives in Japan

    Nintendo’s new console makes its Japanese debut.

  4. Jul 4, 1996 · Internet

    Hotmail puts an inbox in the browser

    Hotmail announces free web-based email, available from July 4, 1996. The original company announcement survives in the NET-HAPPENINGS mailing-list archive.

  5. Jul 5, 1996 · Technology

    Dolly the sheep is born

    Dolly is born at the Roslin Institute in Scotland.

  6. Jul 8, 1996 · Music

    The Spice Girls introduce Wannabe

    The group’s first UK single makes friendship the hook.

  7. Jul 19, 1996 · Sports

    Muhammad Ali lights the Atlanta cauldron

    The opening ceremony supplies the Summer Games with an unforgettable last torchbearer.

  8. Sep 29, 1996 · Games

    Nintendo 64 launches in the U.S.

    Nintendo 64 arrives with Super Mario 64 leading the way.

  9. Nov 15, 1996 · Movies

    Space Jam joins the November lineup

    Basketball and cartoons share a place on the cinema screen.

  10. Nov 23, 1996 · Toys & products

    Tamagotchi makes care portable

    Bandai’s original handheld care toy arrives.

  11. Dec 20, 1996 · Movies

    Scream opens before the holidays

    A December release for the horror film that knows its genre.
