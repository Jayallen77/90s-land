THE DECADE, DAY BY DAY

1998

Clear purple and Bondi blue

The iMac put blue plastic on the desk; Game Boy Color put clear purple in a pocket. Lauryn Hill released her solo debut and Google became a company. France won its home World Cup, and the Good Friday Agreement set out a peace settlement.

the computer becomes room decor · 1998–1999

Bondi blue iMac G3

About this object

A blue shell on the desk, with the hardware partly visible inside.

The original 1998 iMac combines a CRT display and computer in a curved Bondi blue enclosure. The translucent case, handle and integrated form make the desktop into one conspicuous object. Bondi blue identifies this first edition, distinct from the later multi-color range.

the backpack gets a clear shell · 1998–1999

Atomic Purple Game Boy Color

About this object

Clear purple plastic lets you inspect the board before switching the handheld on.

Nintendo’s Game Boy Color, introduced in 1998, adds color to the handheld’s display possibilities while retaining the screen-above-controls arrangement. This reference is the Atomic Purple version, whose translucent shell makes part of the internal structure visible.

three-prong multiplayer sculpture · 1996–1999

Gray Nintendo 64 controller

About this object

Its odd shape and analog stick announced that 3D games would need a different kind of touch.

Nintendo’s original N64 controller offers three grips, a central control stick, a directional cross and buttons in several locations. The three handles propose alternative hand positions; they do not ask for a third hand. Different games make different parts of the pad important.
