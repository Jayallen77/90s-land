Nokia 3210

A pocket phone at the edge of the decade.

The object’s story

The Nokia 3210 is a 1999 handset with a numeric keypad and compact screen. Calls and text messages can reach an individual carrying the phone, rather than only a household at a fixed location. The internal antenna helps define its clean exterior shape.

The keypad does more than dial

Text entry shares numbered keys with letters, so composing a message involves a different rhythm from a full keyboard. Service, network support and available credit or subscription still shape reachability. The handset’s familiar outline should not imply a camera, a modern app platform or the features of later Nokia models.
